// WHO WE ARE

OUR MISSION & STORY

Dedicated to supporting military veterans, law enforcement, first responders, and their families through peer connection, therapeutic outdoor programs, and community integration.

// OUR MISSION

HEALING THROUGH PURPOSE & PEER CONNECTION

Gunny's Ridge is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization established to provide a sanctuary for military veterans and first responders. We offer a supportive environment that fosters personal growth, mental well-being, and community reintegration through hands-on activities, outdoor therapy, and peer-to-peer engagement.

Our goal is to reduce isolation, combat PTSD and suicide risks, and provide practical tools for everyday resilience in a serene rural setting.

// OUR VISION

A LASTING SANCTUARY FOR EVERY HERO

We envision a country where every veteran, police officer, firefighter, paramedic, and dispatcher has immediate access to a trusted, barrier-free community network.

By blending agricultural engagement, woodworking, K9 companionship, and group retreats, Gunny's Ridge strives to be a regional benchmark for holistic recovery and lifelong camaraderie.

// FOUNDED IN LOVING MEMORY

GUNNERY SERGEANT JOHN WILSON

The heart and inspiration behind Gunny's Ridge—a leader, Marine, and combat veteran whose legacy fuels our mission to support heroes and their families.

USMC COMBAT VETERAN
// IN REMEMBRANCE & HONOR

GySgt John "Gunny" Wilson

Gunny’s Ridge was founded in loving memory of Gunnery Sergeant John "Gunny" Wilson—a decorated Marine, devoted community leader, and steadfast friend.

John served honorably in Operation Desert Shield/Desert Storm and Iraqi Freedom. During his service, he survived a devastating roadside bomb attack in Iraq; despite suffering severe injuries, including a broken wrist and a major leg wound, his immediate priority was rescuing and caring for his fellow Marines. For his extraordinary heroism, John was awarded the Purple Heart, the Navy Commendation Medal, and the Combat Action Ribbon, alongside more than 25 other distinguished service decorations.

Beyond the battlefield, John's dedication to service never wavered. As Commander of American Legion Post 952 and an active firefighter with the Hookstown Volunteer Fire Department, he was a pillar of his local community. Known for his unwavering leadership, empathy, and readiness to help anyone in need, John left an indelible mark on every life he touched.

Gunny’s Ridge carries forward his enduring legacy of brotherhood, selflessness, and selfless service.

// OUR ORIGIN

BUILT BY SERVICE, DRIVEN BY PASSION

Transitioning out of active duty military service or stepping away from high-stress emergency response roles presents unique psychological and emotional hurdles. The loss of unit camaraderie and mission structure often creates feelings of detachment and dislocation.

Gunny's Ridge was established in Burgettstown, PA, to bridge that gap. Named in honor of Gunnery Sergeant John Wilson and the military leadership traditions of resilience and troop welfare, our ridge serves as a quiet sanctuary where service members and responders can let down their guard without judgment.

// CORE GUIDING PILLARS

THE VALUES THAT DRIVE OUR WORK

01

Camaraderie & Trust

Rebuilding the tight-knit bond and trust inherent to military and first responder units in a safe, non-judgmental environment.

02

Hands-On Purpose

Restoring a sense of tactical focus and accomplishment through woodworking workshops, farming, and facility maintenance.

03

Holistic Well-being

Supporting emotional, mental, and physical health through K9 pairings, outdoor activities, and active listening groups.

04

Family Inclusivity

Recognizing that service impacts the entire household—extending welcoming events and support circles to spouses and children.

BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Michael Snider

US ARMY VET // PRESIDENT

MSG (Ret) Michael Snider dedicated his career to public service, spending 30 years as a U.S. Army Green Beret and 25 years as a police officer. His military service with the 5th and 19th Special Forces Groups and SOD-E included deployments to Iraq, Afghanistan, Korea, and Europe to build NATO Special Operations capabilities.
