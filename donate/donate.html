GIVING FAQ

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Gunny's Ridge is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. In a live donation flow, qualifying gifts would be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and you would receive a receipt for your records.

Who do these gifts serve?

Gifts support military veterans, law enforcement, first responders, and their families — funding peer healing, hands-on programs, K9 pathways, and retreats at our sanctuary in Burgettstown, PA.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Yes. Monthly giving is meant to be flexible. In production, you would manage or cancel a recurring gift through your receipt email or by contacting Gunny's Ridge directly. This page is a demonstration only.
