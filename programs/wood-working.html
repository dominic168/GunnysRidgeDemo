WOOD WORKING WORKSHOPS

Crafting, furniture making, and hands-on skill building that foster creativity and a sense of accomplishment.

We hold workshops on woodworking, crafting, and furniture making—fostering creativity and mastering new skills. Engaging in the creation of something tangible with a purpose, and working together to bring a vision to fruition, adds meaning and a sense of accomplishment for veterans and first responders.

PROGRAM FAQ

What will I learn?

Basic tool safety, measuring and cutting, joinery fundamentals, finishing techniques, and collaborative project work such as benches, frames, or small furniture pieces.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. Workshops are designed so newcomers can contribute safely while more experienced participants take on advanced tasks.

What should I bring?

Closed-toe shoes and comfortable work clothes. Tools and materials are provided unless otherwise noted for a specific workshop.
