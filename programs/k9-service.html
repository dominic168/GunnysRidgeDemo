K9 SERVICE DOG PROGRAM

Service canine pairing, handler orientation, and animal-assisted support for trauma recovery.

Gunny's Ridge partners with trusted organizations to support medically trained service dog pairings and handler orientation for veterans and first responders. Animal-assisted therapy circles and ongoing peer guidance help participants manage trauma symptoms while building a lasting bond with a trained companion.

PROGRAM FAQ

How do I apply for a service dog?

Complete our intake process and we will connect you with partner organizations that evaluate eligibility, train dogs, and support the matching process.

Is every participant guaranteed a dog?

No. Pairings depend on clinical need, readiness, partner capacity, and successful handler orientation. We guide you through each step transparently.

What ongoing support is available?

Handler education, peer check-ins, and referrals for veterinary and training resources help sustain a healthy working relationship with your K9 partner.
