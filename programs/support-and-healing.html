SUPPORT AND HEALING

Fostering personal growth, mental well-being, and community reintegration through peer-to-peer engagement and active listening.

Transitioning out of high-stress roles presents unique emotional hurdles. The loss of unit camaraderie often creates feelings of detachment. Our Support and Healing program bridges that gap by offering active listening circles, peer mentorship, and mental resilience workshops in a safe, non-judgmental environment.

PROGRAM FAQ

Who is eligible to participate?

This program is strictly available to active and retired military veterans, law enforcement officers, firefighters, paramedics, and emergency dispatchers.

What is an active listening circle?

A structured, confidential group session led by peer facilitators where participants can share their experiences and challenges with others who truly understand the demands of service.

Do I need to sign up in advance?

Yes. To maintain a safe and manageable environment, all participants must complete our secure intake process prior to attending their first session.
